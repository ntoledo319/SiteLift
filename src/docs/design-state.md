# Design State: SiteLift Editorial V3 (The High-Impact Rebirth)

## Project Overview

A complete rejection of "cookie-cutter" SaaS templates in favor of a high-end, art-gallery-level editorial experience. SiteLift V3 is designed to feel visceral, expensive, and technically superior—moving from the "WordPress Death" narrative to "Digital Vitality."

- **Primary Persona:** Small business owners/nonprofits trapped in "WordPress Drag."
- **Success Metric:** A+ Design Grade, 100/100 Lighthouse Performance.
- **Full Brief:** [./docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-05-09-sitelift-v3-brief.md](./docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-05-09-sitelift-v3-brief.md)

## Design Principles

1. **Radical Asymmetry:** Zero tiles, zero boxes. Use overlapping layers and unconventional grid placements.
2. **Massive Typography:** Headlines that bleed off-screen to create physical weight and impact.
3. **Atmospheric Texture:** SVG grain, noise overlays, and light-leak gradients to kill the "flat digital" feel.
4. **Liquid Motion:** All interaction must feel fluid—staggered character reveals and multi-layered parallax depth.
5. **Brand Motif:** The "chopped up" logo as a dynamic, drifting watermark in the background.

## Decisions Log

- **[2026-05-09] Conceptual Pivot:** Rejection of "Sanctuary" vibe (bland) for "High-Impact Vitality" (aggressive/vibrant).
- **[2026-05-09] Pricing Audit:** Flagship "Aegis" tier locked at $8,500; hosting at $20/mo (Vercel Pro).

- **[2026-05-09] Performance Lock:** Committed to Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS with zero dependencies to ensure raw speed.
