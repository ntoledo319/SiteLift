# Design Brief: SiteLift Editorial V3 (The High-Impact Rebirth)

## Objective

Rebuild the SiteLift landing page with maximum visual intensity and editorial elegance. Eliminate the "boring/bland" Sanctuary feel in favor of something vibrant, energetic, and high-performance.

## Rebrand

- **Service Name:** SiteLift (The "Life" of your digital presence).
- **Core Hook:** WordPress is technical death; SiteLift is technical vitality.

## Aesthetic Direction: High-Impact Editorial

- **Visual Mood:** Vibrant, high-speed, and ultra-modern.
- **Layout:** Radical asymmetry. Massive type that bleeds off-screen. Overlapping high-resolution imagery. NO TILES. NO BOXES.
- **Imagery:** Curated, high-resolution architectural and abstract light photography (e.g., Unsplash source IDs). Focus on:
    - Futuristic glass architecture (`photo-1486406146926-c627a92ad1ab`)
    - Vibrant, electric light blurs (`photo-1550684848-fac1c5b4e853`)
    - Minimalist, sun-drenched tech spaces (`photo-1497366754035-f200968a6e72`)
- **Typography:**
    - Headlines: _Fraunces_ (Extra-Bold/Black) — oversized and aggressive.
    - Body: _Inter_ (Regular/Medium) — tight and precise.
- **Color Palette:** Deep Ink (`#050505`) base with high-vibrancy accents: **Electric Violet** (`#8b5cf6`), **Laser Cyan** (`#06b6d4`), and **Sunset Orange** (`#f97316`).

## Performance Mandate

- **Lighthouse:** 100/100.
- **Assets:** WebP optimization for all high-res heros. Vanilla CSS for all animations. Zero JS libraries.

## Accurate Product Data (Verified)

- **Primary Value:** WordPress Exit. $20/mo Hosting. Full Ownership.
- **The $8,500 Quote:** Frame the "Aegis" (now SiteLift standard) as the premium entry point.
- **Tiers:** Arcadia ($4,500), Aegis ($8,500), Odyssey ($14,500), Elysian ($27,500+).
- **Platform Agnostic:** Standard disclosure included.

## Success Criteria

- Feels "Visceral" and "Expensive."
- Zero AI-typical patterns.
- High-conversion narrative flow ("The Breakup" -> "The New Life").
