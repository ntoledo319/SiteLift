Privacy policy.

This SiteLift-specific policy explains how Toledo Technologies LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information at sitelift.toledotechnologies.com.

Last updated: July 15, 2026

Information we collect

The only required public intake field is your email address. If you choose to provide them, we also collect your name, website URL, current platform, project notes, and hidden page or offer context used to route the inquiry.

This site does not currently load client-side analytics or advertising trackers. Its host and security layer may process ordinary request data such as IP address, browser, requested path, and timestamp for delivery, reliability, and abuse prevention.

How we use and share it

We use submitted information to respond to your inquiry, evaluate service fit, provide requested services, and operate and secure the site.

We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for third-party marketing. We may share it with operational service providers, when required by law or needed to protect our rights, or with your consent.

Lead form and your choices

The Fit Check form submits to Toledo Technologies' lead intake API at https://eolkits.com/api/v1/lead. The service stores the submitted fields in Toledo's operating lead database and sends the owner a notification email. It does not enroll you in a newsletter.

Email hello@toledotechnologies.com to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information you submitted, or to contact us without using the form.

Security, storage, and links

We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. SiteLift does not set advertising cookies or use browser storage to build a profile.

External sites have their own privacy practices. This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the revision date here. For privacy questions or requests, email hello@toledotechnologies.com.
